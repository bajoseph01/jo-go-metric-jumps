How It Works

The Big Hand and the Little Hand

The long hand counts minutes — each number on the clock is worth 5 minutes. The short hand points at the hour.

Three Levels

  1. Whole & Half — o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to.
  2. Five minutes — count in fives round the clock.
  3. One minute — every single minute.

Three Steps

  1. Read the big hand. It points at a number worth 5 minutes each.
  2. Read the little hand. It sits just past the hour.
  3. Type the time. Like 3:45. Then check!

🚀 The first five minutes

This is exactly what a child sees when they first open Tick⚡Tock. Walk through it once, then hand over the iPad.

1 · First play — the teaching intro

On their very first game, every child gets a one-time overlay before any question:

  1. Look at the BIG hand. Each number on the clock is worth 5 minutes (5, 10, 15…).
  2. Look at the little hand. It points just past the hour.
  3. Type the time — like 3:45 — and press Check!

They tap Got it — let’s go! ⚡ and it never shows again for that learner.

2 · The levels

Three levels unlock in order. Each shows its own reading rule under the clock:

  • Whole & Half — o’clock, half past, quarter past & to.
  • Five minutes — count in fives round the clock (unlocks after 5 first-tries).
  • One minute — each little tick is 1 minute (unlocks after 5 first-tries).

3 · Worksheets & reporting

Under 📝 Worksheets you can generate a sheet of clocks (answer key hidden by default, one tap to show), print it, or export a PDF. 📊 Reports shows each learner’s attempts and first-try accuracy per level.
