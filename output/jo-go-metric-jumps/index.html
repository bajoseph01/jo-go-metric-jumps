How It Works

The Golden Rule

× = RIGHT  ·  ÷ = LEFT  ·  ZEROES = JUMPS

Down the ladder → multiply (the number grows). Up the ladder → divide (the number shrinks). Same amount, new unit!

Each gap on the ladder has its own jump size: km → m is a 1000-jump, m → cm is a 100-jump, cm → mm is a 10-jump.

Three Steps

  1. Which way are we going? To a SMALLER unit → the number gets bigger → multiply. To a BIGGER unit → the number gets smaller → divide.
  2. Count the zeroes. They tell you how many places the comma moves — 10 has one zero, 100 has two, 1000 has three.
  3. Move the comma. Drag it across the place-value track. Watch the zeroes appear!

Same Amount, New Unit

2,5 m = 250 cm  ·  2,5 kg = 2 500 g  ·  2,5 L = 2 500 mL

The amount did not change. We just used a smaller unit, so the number got bigger. 100 centimetres fit into 1 metre, 1 000 grams into 1 kilogram, 1 000 millilitres into 1 litre.

Check It Makes Sense

After converting, ask yourself: does this answer look like the real world? A door is about 2 m tall. 2,5 m = 0,025 cm would be smaller than an ant — something is wrong!
