How It Works

The Golden Rule

× = RIGHT  ·  ÷ = LEFT  ·  ZEROES = JUMPS

Going down the ladder means multiply. Going up means divide.

Each gap has its own power of ten: km → m is ×1000, m → cm is ×100, cm → mm is ×10.

Three Steps

  1. Find the operation. Smaller unit → bigger number → multiply. Bigger unit → smaller number → divide.
  2. Count the jumps. The zeroes in 10, 100 or 1000 tell you how many places the comma moves.
  3. Move the comma. Drag it across the place-value track. Watch the zeroes appear!

Same Length, New Unit

2,5 m = 250 cm

The rope did not get longer. We just used a smaller unit, so the number got bigger. 100 centimetres fit into 1 metre.

Check It Makes Sense

After converting, ask yourself: does this answer look like the real world? A door is about 2 m tall. 2,5 m = 0,025 cm would be smaller than an ant — something is wrong!
